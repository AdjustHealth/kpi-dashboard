Leave any test blank to leave it out of the report — same as everywhere else in this form. Enter Bodyweight above (in the IMTP row) to get accurate Strong/Avg/Developing ratings on the weighted lifts below — they're scored as a multiple of bodyweight, not raw kg.
No barbell tests available for Youth 1 (8–12) — per the ASCA Position Stand on Resistance Training for Children and Youth, this age band trains and tests using bodyweight, bands and machine exercises only. Bodyweight tests below are shown unrated (no age-specific norm published) — use the ASCA competency benchmark noted under each as a pass guide.
Youth 2 (13–18): Bench Press is rated as %-bodyweight per ASCA's youth progression gates (40% BW at 13 → 70% BW boys / 50% BW girls by 15+). Squat & Deadlift are shown for tracking only and left unrated — ASCA does not publish a relative-strength benchmark for these lifts in youth, and recommends they only be introduced under coaching accredited to at least ASCA Level 2, using an 8–15RM training range rather than maximal loads. Olympic lift variants are not included in the youth battery.
